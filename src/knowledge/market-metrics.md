# Market Statistics Metrics

## Median Close Price

Median close price is the middle sale price after qualifying closed sales are sorted from lowest to highest.

The Week 5 market statistics implementation calculates the median from historical sales records in california_sold.

## Average Close Price

Average close price is the arithmetic mean of qualifying sale prices.

## Average Price Per Square Foot

Average price per square foot compares property sale price with living area.

## Average Days on Market

Average Days on Market measures the average amount of time qualifying listings remained on the market before sale.

## Inventory

Inventory represents the number of active properties available in a selected market.

## List-to-Close Price Ratio

The list-to-close price ratio compares a property's final close price with its listing price.

A value near 100 percent means the property sold near its asking price.
