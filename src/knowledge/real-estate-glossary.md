# Real Estate Glossary

## DOM

DOM stands for Days on Market. It measures how many days a property has been actively listed for sale before going under contract, being sold, or otherwise leaving active status.

A lower average DOM can indicate stronger buyer demand, while a higher DOM can indicate that homes are taking longer to sell.

## Comparable Sales

Comparable sales, often called comps, are recently sold properties that are similar to a subject property in characteristics such as location, size, age, condition, bedrooms, bathrooms, and property type.

Comps are commonly used to estimate market value.

## Escrow

Escrow is a process in which a neutral third party holds funds, documents, and other items associated with a real estate transaction until agreed conditions are satisfied.

## Cap Rate

Capitalization rate, or cap rate, is commonly used to evaluate income-producing real estate.

It is generally calculated as:

Cap Rate = Net Operating Income / Property Value

## List-to-Close Price Ratio

The list-to-close price ratio compares a property's final sale price with its listing price.

A ratio close to 100 percent means the property sold near its asking price.
